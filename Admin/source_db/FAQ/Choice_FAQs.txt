Q: Who provides the Choice Protect360 insurance policy?
A: Choice Protect360 is provided and underwritten by BigTapp Pte. Ltd. (Co. Reg. No. 201229558W), a member of the BigTapp Group. This policy is protected under the Policy Owner's Protection Scheme administered by the Singapore Deposit Insurance Corporation (SDIC).

Q: Who can buy Choice Protect360?
A: You are eligible to purchase this Policy if You are between the ages of eighteen (18) and sixty-five (65) years old. You must be a Singapore citizen, Singapore permanent resident, expatriate or foreigner who holds a valid pass and is ordinarily residing in Singapore.

Q: Does this Policy have age restrictions?
A: Yes, the main policyholder must be between 18 and 65 years old at the start of the policy, renewable up to 70 years. For children, coverage is from 1 to 18 years old, or up to 25 if studying full-time. Age calculated as at last birthday.

Q: What does Choice Protect360 cover?
A: Choice Protect360’s key benefits are: Up to $500,000 coverage in the event of Accidental Death and Permanent Disablement No Claim Discount of up to 10% of the premium Up to $10,000 of Accidental Medical Reimbursement (including infectious diseases) Up to $250 Daily Hospital Income in the event that you sustain injury and was hospitalized Purchase Protection of $5,000 and free dependent child cover (for Accidental Death and Permanent Disablement, Accidental Medical Reimbursement, Mobility Aid & Ambulance Services Reimbursement)

Q: What are the Choice Protect360 exclusions?
A: You can find out about our exclusions for Choice Protect360 here.

Q: How does the No Claim Discount (NCD) work?
A: You can receive a 5% discount after one (1) year of no claim, and a maximum of 10% after two (2) or more consecutive years with no claim.

Q: Does Choice Protect360 cover child(ren)?
A: Yes, unmarried children who are between 1 to 18 years old (or up to 25 years old if studying full time) will be covered for free, as long as a Family Plan is purchased.

Q: Is there a discount if I choose a family plan?
A: Yes. When you opt for a family plan with spouse coverage, you’ll enjoy a 10% discount on the Personal Accident premium. Plus, your child(ren) is automatically covered at no additional cost for Personal Accident benefits (for Accidental Death and Permanent Disablement, Accidental Medical Reimbursement, Mobility Aid & Ambulance Services Reimbursement).
