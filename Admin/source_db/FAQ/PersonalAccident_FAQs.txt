Q: What is the Automatic Increase of Benefit Limit in Family Protect360?
A: Family Protect360 has an Automatic Increase of Benefit Limit feature for Section 1 (Accidental Death & Permanent Disablement). If no claims have been made and coverage is continuous, the benefit limit increases automatically upon renewal: 5% after the first policy year, 10% after the second policy year, and 15% from the third policy year onwards. Conditions: there must be no interruption of cover, and no claim must have been made prior to the applicable Anniversary Date.

Q: What happens to the Automatic Increase of Benefit Limit if a claim is made?
A: If any claim is made under the Policy, the automatic increase in the Benefit Limit for Section 1 (Accidental Death & Permanent Disablement) shall cease totally and immediately from the Anniversary Date following the date of the event giving rise to the claim. The increase stops from the next Anniversary Date, not retroactively.

Q: What does Section 2 of Family Protect360 cover?
A: Section 2 of Family Protect360 covers Accidental Medical Reimbursement. It pays up to the Benefit Limit for Medical Expenses incurred as a result of an Injury, provided fees are necessarily and reasonably incurred from a Registered Medical Practitioner. It also covers: Chinese Physician or Chiropractor treatment expenses (up to 50% of the Benefit Limit), Medical Expenses from Infectious Disease (provided no claim is made within the Waiting Period), and Ambulance charges to transport you to a Hospital (up to $200 per injury).

Q: Who can apply for this Policy?
A: You can apply this Policy if you are between the age of 18 to 65 years old. You must be a Singapore Citizen, Singapore Permanent resident or a foreigner holding a valid pass issued by Singapore government and is residing in Singapore.

Q: Can my children included in the Family Protect360?
A: Yes, unmarried children who are between 1 to 18 years old (or up to 25 years old if studying full time) will be covered for free, as long as Family Plan is purchased.

Q: How does this Policy pay out for Income Protector?
A: In the event of Permanent Total Disablement, this Policy will pay a monthly benefit up to the maximum sum assured stated. However you must be gainfully employed at the time of Accident.

Q: Does this Policy premium increase with age?
A: No. The Policy premium is not affected by age and does not increase as you get older.

Q: Do I need to inform BigTapp upon the annual renewal of this Policy?
A: No. If you pay the premium and observe the terms and conditions of your Policy, it will be automatically renewed every year as long as its approved by us.
