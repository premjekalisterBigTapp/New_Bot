Under the insurance policy's Critical Illness Benefit (Section A), the Early Stage Critical Illness and Advanced Stage Critical Illness coverage is 100% of Sum Insured with coverage options ranging from $50,000 to $300,000.
Additionally, under Section A, Angioplasty and Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery is covered at 10% of Sum Insured with a maximum limit of up to S$25,000.
Under the insurance policy's Extra ICU Benefit (Section B), additional cash is payable if you are in the ICU for 4 or more consecutive days in one admission at 20% of Sum Insured with a maximum limit of up to S$25,000.
Additionally, under Section B, this Extra ICU Benefit top-up does not reduce your critical illness cover.
